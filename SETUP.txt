Warehouse Opportunity Finder: setup

1. Create the database
   - Go to sheets.google.com, then File > Import > Upload "Opportunity Finder Database.xlsx".
     Choose "Replace spreadsheet". Keep the tab names (Opportunities, Technologies, ABC example).
   - Click Share. Under General access choose "Anyone with the link" with role Viewer.
     (The website needs this to read the data. Viewers can't change anything.)
   - Add each person who maintains the data as an Editor. They log in with their
     Google account email and password. Anyone without a Google account can create
     one at accounts.google.com using their existing work email.

2. Connect the website
   - Copy the sheet ID from its address: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/<SHEET ID>/edit
   - Open config.js in a text editor and paste it between the quotes: sheetId: "<SHEET ID>"

3. Publish the website (free)
   - Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
   - Netlify gives you a public link. Sign up (free) to keep it and pick a name.
   - To update the site files later, drag the folder onto the same site's Deploys page.

Editing
   - Editors change the Google Sheet directly. Changes appear on the website on the next
     page refresh (Google can take a minute or two).
   - The "How to edit" tab in the sheet explains the columns.
   - data-snapshot.js is a saved copy used only if the sheet can't be reached.
